# StudyMate Product Description

## Short description

StudyMate is an accessible university planner that helps students stay organised, keep up with coursework, manage class activities, and recover quickly when they cannot attend class.

## Product overview

Students often manage their academic lives across disconnected systems: learning platforms, messaging groups, email, calendars, lecture recordings, and personal notes. This makes it easy to miss deadlines, lose important resources, or fall behind after an absence.

StudyMate creates one supportive study space for the work around university life. Students can see their priorities, track assignments, organise classes, save catch-up requests, and share a view of their plan with classmates or lecturers.

The product is designed to reduce cognitive load rather than add another complicated system. It uses plain language, clear visual hierarchy, responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly controls, dark mode, reduced-motion support, and a calm olive visual identity.

## Target users

- University students managing multiple subjects and activities
- Students who miss class because of illness, work, disability, caring responsibilities, or other circumstances
- Lecturers who need a simple way to share catch-up materials and respond to student support requests
- Classmates who want to share notes, resources, or view-only planning information

## Core experience

1. A student signs in or creates a StudyMate space.
2. The dashboard highlights tasks, upcoming work, today’s schedule, and current progress.
3. The student adds assignments, classes, notes, events, and study activities.
4. If they miss class, they open the Catch-up Hub, explain what support they need, and optionally select a screenshot or file.
5. Students can review shared class materials and create a view-only link to share their planner.

## Value proposition

StudyMate helps students move from “I have fallen behind” to “I know what to do next.” It combines planning, class context, catch-up support, and sharing in a focused interface that is designed to be approachable for everyone.

## Current MVP

The current release is a frontend prototype with browser-local storage. It demonstrates the product experience without requiring a backend, account service, or database. This makes it easy to preview and deploy as a static site while the production data model and privacy approach are validated.

## Future direction

A production version can add university SSO, lecturer workspaces, secure file storage, real-time support requests, calendar and learning-platform integrations, email or push reminders, captions and transcripts, multilingual support, and institution-level analytics that protect student privacy.
